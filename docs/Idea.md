### 🌱 *Student-Centered Monitoring*
- *Simple ID System:* Use QR codes or numeric labels that are easy for students to generate and attach to seedlings.  
- *Photo + Notes:* Each scan should allow students to add a quick photo and short notes (e.g., “added fertilizer today,” “leaves turning yellow”).  
- *Growth Milestones:* Track fruit-bearing stages (flowering, fruit set, ripening) since these are critical for wildlings.

---

### 📊 *Data & Research Integration*
- *Basic Growth Metrics:* Height, stem diameter, number of leaves, and fruit count — easy for students to measure.  
- *Weekly & Monthly Graphs:* Auto-generate simple charts showing growth trends and fruit development.  
- *Export to Excel:* Students can download their data for research defense or class reports.

---

### 🔔 *Engagement & Learning*
- *Reminders:* Notifications for watering, fertilizing, or checking sunlight exposure.  
- *Gamification:* Badges like “Fruit First!” (first fruit recorded) or “Consistent Care” (weekly updates for a month).  
- *Peer Sharing:* Students can share progress with classmates or teachers, encouraging collaboration.

---

### 🌍 *Practical & Sustainability Features*
- *Species-Specific Care Guides:* Built-in tips for Mango, Guyabano, Jackfruit, Coconut, etc.  
- *Survival Tracker:* Show percentage of seedlings that survive over time, motivating students to care for their trees.  
- *Campus Map:* Visualize where each wildling is planted, making it easier to locate and monitor.

---

### 🔐 *Technical Enhancements*
- *Offline Mode:* Students can record data even without internet, syncing later.  
- *Cloud Backup:* Prevents data loss if phones are reset.  
- *Role-Based Access:* Teachers can view all student entries, while students only see their own.
